## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an ultra-premium, Apple-inspired e-commerce and interactive vector customization environment tailored for commercial print buyers, brand directors, and print production specialists. The visual narrative combines the tactile poise of high-end editorial paper goods with the crisp, architectural clarity of modern desktop utility software.

### Tone & Personality
- **Architectural & Authoritative:** High precision, uncompromising pixel alignment, and calm, confident authority.
- **Tactile Luxury:** Digital surfaces replicate physical presswork—calibrated paper textures, controlled light bounce, and micro-reflections.
- **Frictionless Utility:** Complex commercial print operations (bleed tolerances, CMYK overprint simulations, paper stock weights) are distilled into intuitive, stepped interactions.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Direction
A sophisticated fusion of **Apple Modernism** and **Studio Precision Utility**. Clean, neutral, high-key canvas layouts (`#FFFFFF`, `#F8FAFC`) framed by crisp 1px hairpins, subtle ambient diffused drop-shadows, and frosted glass toolbars. Deep Navy provides weight and contrast for primary navigation and technical overlays, while Electric Blue acts as a laser-precise focal point for interactive affordances and production-safe validation states.

## Colors

The palette is engineered to provide high dynamic contrast, ensuring product mockups, finishes, and tactile paper stocks take center stage without visual competition.

### Core Architecture
- **Primary Canvas & Neutrals:**
  - `Canvas Main`: `#F8FAFC` — Crisp, cool foundational backdrop that mimics natural daylight over neutral gray studio setups.
  - `Surface Elevated`: `#FFFFFF` — Pure white for interactive cards, inspectors, and product tilt stages.
  - `Surface Subdued`: `#F1F5F9` — Inset parameter panels, ruler bases, and secondary controls.
  - `Hairline Border`: `#E2E8F0` — Subtle 1px structural boundaries.

- **Deep Navy (Contrast & Typography):**
  - `Navy 950`: `#0F172A` — Primary text, dark tool headers, high-impact CTA buttons, and modal scrims.
  - `Navy 800`: `#1E293B` — Secondary titles, hover states on dark controls, and vector tooltips.
  - `Slate 500`: `#64748B` — Secondary captions, dimensional labels, and inactive icon states.

- **Electric Blue (Precision Accents & Vectors):**
  - `Electric Blue`: `#2563eb` — Primary interactive vectors, selection bounding boxes, active stepper circles, and selected finish pills.
  - `Electric Blue Light`: `#3B82F6` — Hover vectors, transform handles, and live guide markers.
  - `Blue Wash`: `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.08)` — Subtle selection fills, focus halos, and active row backgrounds.

- **Emerald Green (Production Badges & Value):**
  - `Emerald 500`: `#10B981` — Volume savings callouts, instant proofing approvals, "Print Ready" safe-zone verifications, and eco-certified paper badges.
  - `Emerald 50`: `#ECFDF5` — Background wash for proof verification chips and bulk pricing highlights.

- **Specialized Customizer Safety Colors:**
  - `Bleed Margin`: `#EF4444` (0.5px dotted crimson boundary line).
  - `Safe Zone Area`: `#3B82F6` (0.5px dashed cyan guide line).
  - `Trim Edge`: `#0F172A` (1px solid black boundary line).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances modern Swiss restraint with precise technical telemetry. 

- **Primary Typeface (`Inter`):** Selected for its hyper-neutral optical balance, tall x-height, and Apple SF Pro-adjacent aesthetic. Headlines utilize tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and confident medium-to-semibold weights to command visual hierarchy without brute force.
- **Telemetry & Measurement Typeface (`JetBrains Mono`):** Dedicated to technical readouts, dimensional rulers, canvas zoom percentages, CMYK/Pantone codes, paper weight indicators (`350 GSM`, `16 pt Silk`), and bleed delta coordinates.
- **Hierarchy Application:**
  - Product titles and hero pricing leverage tight, crisp Inter weights.
  - Step headings (`01 Finish`, `02 Stock Weight`) pair an uppercase `label-caps` prefix with an `Inter` title.
  - Interactive inputs and numeric counters are strictly rendered with tabular figures (`tnum`) enabled via OpenType features to prevent jitter during real-time size updates.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an intentional split-screen studio architectural model, bridging seamless product configuration with full-bleed vector customization.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):**
  - PDP uses an asymmetric 12-column layout: Columns 1–7 host the fixed-height 3D interactive tilt stage and material detail view; Columns 8–12 host the sticky, stepped specification and pricing configurator.
  - Studio Customizer Modal uses an edge-to-edge application chrome layout: 48px persistent top toolstrip, 64px floating vertical left vector rail, center fluid artboard stage framed by 24px metric rulers, and 360px collapsible right property inspector.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):**
  - 8-column layout. The 3D stage anchors the top with a 16:10 aspect ratio; configuration switches to accordion blocks below.
- **Mobile (< 768px):**
  - Single column with `1rem` safe margins. Floating sticky bottom bar displaying aggregate price and "Customize Artwork" CTA.

### Spatial Rhythms
- Spacing strictly scales along a 4px/8px baseline grid.
- In-component micro-spacing (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px) controls nested selector tiles (e.g., swatch chips inside material cards).
- Macro-spacing (`space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px) segregates functional configuration steps, avoiding visual noise.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through high-aperture studio lighting, subtle material diffusion, and crisp hairline boundaries rather than high-contrast drops.

### Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` flat surface.
2. **Level 1 (Cards, Selectors, Panels):** Pure `#FFFFFF` resting surface with a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) and an ambient micro-shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
3. **Level 2 (Interactive 3D Stage & Floating Toolbars):** Elevated surfaces utilize dual-layer lighting to simulate physical studio depth:
   - Box shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
   - Toolbars employ Apple-style frosted glass: `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82); backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(180%); border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`.
4. **Level 3 (Vector Customizer Modals & Overlays):**
   - High-elevation studio scrim: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`.
   - Floating panels and dialogs: `0 25px 50px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework is calculated and disciplined, reflecting the clean industrial bevels of premium hardware.

- **Primary Geometry:** Base corner radius is `8px` (`0.5rem`), scaling to `16px` (`1rem`) for larger surface cards, 3D viewport viewports, and modal dialogs.
- **Pills & Caps:** Reserved strictly for status badges (`Eco Recycled`, `Save 28%`), measurement pills, and primary action buttons.
- **Micro-Elements:** Configuration swatch buttons and tool icons maintain `6px` radius to maintain a crisp, surgical tool aesthetic.
- **Studio Handles:** Vector selection bounding boxes use crisp `4px` square control handles with a 1.5px `#2563EB` stroke and `#FFFFFF` core.

## Components

### 1. Interactive 3D Mockup Stage
- **Canvas Container:** Rounded-xl (`16px`) container framed in a 1px `#E2E8F0` border with a subtle gradient vignette (`#FFFFFF` to `#F1F5F9`).
- **Interactive Affordance:** Center cursor reveals a floating glass pill (`Rotate 360°` + gyro icon). Dynamic specular light reflection reacts to pointer hover coordinates across paper surface finishes (matte, foil, spot UV gloss).
- **Stage Footbar:** Transparent dock containing zoom controls, paper texture toggle (`Grid`, `Realistic Press`, `CMYK Proof`), and a finish gloss angle slider.

### 2. Stepped PDP Configurator
- **Step Containers:** Numbered index pill (`01`, `02`, `03`) in `label-numeric` format. Active steps are accented with a `#2563EB` left border line.
- **Selection Tiles:** High-density option grids (e.g., Paper Weight: *Soft Velvet 350 GSM*, *Heavy Kraft 450 GSM*). Inactive tiles use `#FFFFFF` with `#E2E8F0` borders; active tiles transition to a 1.5px `#2563EB` outline with an imperceptible blue wash background and a top-right verified checkmark.
- **Dynamic Pricing Engine:** Instantaneous total calculator dock with line-item volume breakdown. Green discount chips (`Save $124`) display an animated count-up on tier transition.

### 3. Vector Studio Customizer Modal
- **Metric Rulers:** Fixed 24px horizontal and vertical rulers rendered in `#F1F5F9` with precise 1mm/0.125in tick marks and dynamic pointer tracking indicators in `#2563EB`.
- **Artboard Canvas:** Centered vector workspace with clear multi-line boundaries:
  - Outer Red boundary (`#EF4444`): 3mm Bleed margin.
  - Middle Black boundary (`#0F172A`): Final Cut / Trim line.
  - Inner Cyan boundary (`#3B82F6`): Live / Text Safe area.
- **Studio Floating Toolstrip:** Detached pill-shaped glass container holding vector tools: Select (`V`), Text Tool (`T`), Shape Library (`U`), Asset Upload (`O`), and Layer Order. Active tool is highlighted in `#0F172A` with inverted white iconography.
- **Inspector Panel (Right):** Collapsible sidebar hosting live vector properties (X/Y coordinates, scale, bleed validation, color separation warnings, and CMYK mismatch notifications).

### 4. Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Studio Button:** Navy fill (`#0F172A`), white text, `8px` radius, hover state shifts to `#1E293B` with a gentle `translateY(-1px)` lift.
- **Customize Artwork CTA:** High-luminance Electric Blue fill (`#2563EB`), white text, glowing focus-ring `0 0 0 4px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.2)`.
- **Secondary Ghost Controls:** Transparent background, 1px border `#CBD5E1`, text `#1E293B`, background fills to `#F1F5F9` on hover.

### 5. Chips, Badges & Verification Tags
- **Savings Chip:** `#ECFDF5` emerald tint, `#10B981` bold text, rounded-full, with an icon showing production cost reduction.
- **Flight-Check Badges:** Real-time upload analyzer badge showing `300 DPI: Ready` (Green) or `Low Res Warning` (Amber, `#F59E0B`).

### 6. Inputs & Quantities
- **Numeric Stepper:** Compact grouped element with `-` and `+` buttons flanking a tabular JetBrains Mono value input. Zero-latency recalculation with production run discounts visible below.
